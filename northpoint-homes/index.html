The problem

What makes selling a home so stressful?

Most of the stress comes from three gaps. A plan closes each one.

  1. Gap 1 of 3

    Not sure what it's worth

    One agent says one number, a website says another. Price it too high and the home sits. Too low and you give money away.

  2. Gap 2 of 3

    Months on the market

    Every extra month means another mortgage payment, more taxes and insurance, and buyers who start to wonder what's wrong with it.

  3. Gap 3 of 3

    Agents who go quiet

    The sign goes up, then the updates stop. You hear about showings from the lockbox app, and feedback never arrives.

Commission in plain words

  1. You pay nothing up front.
  2. The listing commission is a percentage of the final sale price. It comes out of the sale money at closing.
  3. Pay for the buyer's agent is separate. You decide whether to offer it, and how much, before we list.
  4. If the home doesn't sell, you owe no listing commission.
  5. Commission is always negotiable.
